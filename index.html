SunSunBus (公車順順行)

2026

SunSunBus turns passengers' phones into sensors that measure how smooth a bus ride is, detecting hard braking, sharp turns and jerk.

I built it largely on my own at the Civic Debug Hackathon (市政 Debug 松) in Taipei: a no-install PWA, a gamified version, Taipei open bus data, an ESP32 sensor box and Python analysis.

It placed 6th of 34 teams in the public vote, and I demoed it live on stage to over 10,000 concurrent online viewers, including a Taipei mayoral candidate.

Rearia Hai

2019 – 2020

Hai is a Bluetooth earphone designed for hearing aid and cochlear implant users (via T-Coil) that works just as well for people with typical hearing.

I co-founded Rearia Co., Ltd. and served as Product Manager and CTO, leading product R&D end to end: running 25 in-depth prototype trials with users, sourcing and managing an outsourced engineering team and the OEM manufacturer, and coordinating designers, suppliers and customers.

I also worked on pricing and marketing. Our 2020 Indiegogo campaign reached 1,000,000 people and raised NT$150K from 22 backers, falling short of its pre-order goal.

Flexible Underactuated Robotic Hand

Feb 2017 – Jan 2018

Flexible Underactuated Robotic Hand is a four-fingered adaptive robotic hand.

It fully supports the Robot Operating System (ROS) and can be mounted on robots such as NEXTAGE and Baxter.

The hand was built as a platform for testing deep reinforcement learning and other machine learning methods that let robots learn by themselves.

It was a group project in collaboration with a Japanese robotics research lab.

Documentation Assistant Lamp

May 2016 – Jan 2019

DA Lamp is a robotic lamp that automatically photographs every key step of a maker's or designer's work and uploads the photos to a personal documentation database.

It is built from laser-cut frames, 3D-printed parts and cast servo arms, so any makerspace or fab lab can build one quickly from the open-source design.

DA Lamp moves autonomously with 5 degrees of freedom and is controlled by an Android phone mounted on the lampshade, whose built-in camera records as the lamp moves.

The software work focused on object recognition and interactive machine learning.

Rearia (Prototype)

2016

Rearia is a set of redesigned earbuds that let hearing aid users enjoy high-quality music through T-Coil mode.

My friend Yen-Tsen Liu started designing Rearia because his hearing impairment kept him from enjoying good music wherever he went. My role was researching materials, components and T-Coil mode.

The prototype delivered noticeably better sound quality, and this work later led to Rearia Hai.

FabDoc

Dec 2015 – Aug 2017

FabDoc is a web app for makers who want to document their projects step by step.

It borrows the idea of Git-style version control: with a commit-based workflow, makers capture each step as they go and add details after the work is done.

To help open-source hardware projects scale, FabDoc's roadmap included forkable branches, team collaboration and sharing.

Web App GitHub

At each key step, all you need to record is a photo and a short commit, even while your hands are busy making.

Afterwards, you can add details in the browser and even embed each step in your own site.

BatDrone

2015

BatDrone is an app that helps people avoid crime hotspots and stay safe on their way home.

It used crime data from the Taipei City Government's open data platform. Development was suspended because the data was not maintained in the detail we needed.

BatDrone was my first Android app. The project started at a g0v hackathon in Taiwan and was also presented at a weekly meeting of Code for San Francisco.

hackpad GitHub

With location access on, the app sends a push notification when the user approaches a crime hotspot.

3D-tracking Abduction Game

2014 – 2015

3D-tracking Abduction Game is a low-cost UFO abduction game with a 3D interface, built in Processing.

It uses a DIY 3D controller originally designed by Kyle McDonald on Instructables, made from just three sheets of aluminum foil and an Arduino. Processing streams the player-controlled UFO and panicking humans over UDP to an 8×8×8 L3D LED cube.

It was my first Arduino project.
